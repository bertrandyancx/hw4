# Storefront design

- Added an editorial collegiate palette: Yale navy anchors trust, warm rust adds energy, and parchment backgrounds keep the shop approachable.
- Added serif display typography, eyebrow markers, arch-and-seal artwork, and a sticky glassy nav to give the storefront a distinct campus identity.
- Product cards now feel tactile: real catalogue images zoom gently, cards lift on hover, and a “View piece” cue makes the next action obvious.
- Product detail pages get a framed editorial image treatment and clearer price, description, and stock hierarchy.
- The concierge now has a warmer gradient header, animated open state, and subtle card motion so it feels like part of the shop rather than a separate utility.
- Motion is restrained and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, helping the site feel alive without getting in the way of browsing or purchasing.

These details create a memorable browse-and-discover rhythm: strong visual anchors invite exploration, clear affordances reduce hesitation, and the consistent navy/rust system makes product and chat actions feel trustworthy.
