Storefront evidence

Three checks from the running React/Vite site and FastAPI-backed shop. Open this file directly; each screenshot is stored beside it in app_check_images/.

1. Chat checks inventory and price

The concierge reports the catalogue price ($32.00), total stock (112), and size quantities for the 2025 Yale vs Harvard T-Shirt. These values match the database-backed product and inventory records.

2. Category question produces dynamic product cards

After “What hoodies do you have?”, the chat panel renders product cards with real catalogue images, names, prices, and short descriptions. Each card is linked to the existing single-product detail route.

3. Problem 9 usability improvement: search and sort

The Products page is filtered to “hoodie,” shows the result count, and is sorted from low to high price. This makes a large catalogue easier to scan while preserving the clickable product detail flow.
